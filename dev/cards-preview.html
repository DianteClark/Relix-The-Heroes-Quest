Card Concepts

Three directions, same four cards. Tilt any card with your finger.

A. Gilded Relic

Premium collectible: a polished gold frame, a jewel coded by card type, a ribbon nameplate and a parchment rules box. Legendary cards turn holographic.

B. Inked Comic

Matches the hand-drawn art: chunky ink borders, halftone dots, and characters bursting out of the top of the card. Loud, fun, and readable from across the table.

C. Dungeon Stone

An evolution of your current cards: carved stone, glowing crystal corners, an arched torch-lit window, and the boxed first letter and dragon emblem you designed.

Concept only, not in the game yet. The potion and pit pictures are drawn in code, as all 46 treasure and trap pictures would be.
