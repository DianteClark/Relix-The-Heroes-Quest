Card Concepts II

Living, glowing, animated. Touch a card to tilt it.

A. Torchlit

Every card is a window into the dungeon: a stone archway, flickering torches, drifting fog and rising embers, with the character stepping out of the frame lit by the firelight. Stats sit on glowing rune-stones.

B. Soul Forge

Obsidian cards with molten energy flowing through cracks in the card's type color. Spinning sigils, orbiting sparks and characters floating in their own aura. The most "magic item" of the three.

C. Shrine Glass

A gothic gold arch with a stained-glass window in the card's color, sweeping light beams and floating dust. The character is backlit like a relic on an altar, and the name sits on a scroll.

Concepts only, not in the game. Everything moves in the live page. The potion and pit are drawn in code, as all 46 treasure and trap pictures would be.
